YOU SAY LOVE IS FAKE

Well, I know I was only 5 when I first said I liked you. I know at that age I probably didn’t even understand what love really meant.

But still somehow, that little feeling never really went away. Instead, as I grew older, it grew with me

Maybe you call love fake because you haven’t seen what it looks like when someone chooses the same person, again and again, for years.

You were a part of my childhood, a part of my growing up, and somehow, even after all these years, you're still someone who holds a special place in my heart.

I don't know what the future has planned for us, and I don't want to pretend that I have all the answers. I just know that if someone had asked the little five-year-old me what he wanted, he probably would've smiled and said your name.
